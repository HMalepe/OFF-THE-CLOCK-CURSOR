The hosts

Peter Mehlape is co-founder of Off The Clock, the leadership and wellbeing podcast he started with his son, Mahlatse. A conversation between them turned into a show: sit with CEOs, founders and leaders, and ask about the part the title leaves out.

Rest, stress, family, sleep, and how a person actually lives while doing a demanding job. Peter is also the author of Winning in Africa: Your Next 8 Moves for Business Success in Africa, with a foreword by Shameel Joosub, Vodacom group CEO.

From the show

Myth or fact?

Three lines Off The Clock keeps coming back to.

Rest isn’t a weakness

The show exists so executive rest, stress and mental wellness are not treated as off-limits. Burnout is not a strategy. If leadership is going to last, there has to be room for the person behind the title.

FAQ

Before you press play

What is Off The Clock?

A leadership and wellbeing podcast. It was founded to demystify the CEO and talk about executive rest, stress and the person behind the title — not strategy decks.

Who hosts it?

Peter Mehlape and his son, Mahlatse Mehlape. Peter is also the author of Winning in Africa.

Where do I watch?

Episodes are on YouTube. Episode 1 is linked from this page. New conversations are announced with the newsletter.

What is Winning in Africa?

Peter’s book: Your Next 8 Moves for Business Success in Africa. The foreword is by Shameel Joosub, Vodacom group CEO.

How do I reach the show?

Peter is on LinkedIn. That is the public door for now — there is no separate inbox on this page yet.
