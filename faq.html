FAQ

The short answers

What is Off The Clock?

A leadership and wellbeing podcast. It was founded to demystify the CEO and talk about executive rest, stress and the person behind the title — not strategy decks.

Who hosts it?

Peter Mehlape and his son, Mahlatse Mehlape. Peter is also the author of Winning in Africa.

Where do I watch?

On the Off The Clock YouTube channel. The latest episodes are linked from the home page. New conversations are announced with the newsletter.

What is Winning in Africa?

Peter’s book: Your Next 8 Moves for Business Success in Africa. The foreword is by Shameel Joosub, Vodacom group CEO.

How do I reach the show?

Peter is on LinkedIn. That is the public door for now — there is no separate inbox on this page yet.
